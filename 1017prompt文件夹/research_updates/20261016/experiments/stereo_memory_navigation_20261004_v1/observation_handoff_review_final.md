Personally reproduced five identical calls at `time_s=18.0`, XY speed `.01`, and 100 current provisional hazard points: `low_count=5` and handoff becomes true, despite there being only one distinct observation timestamp. A future caller that retries the same capture could qualify prematurely. Before reuse in a caller that can repeat captures, count distinct observation timestamps or reject duplicate samples, with a literal regression fixture.
