# Physical short-horizon primitive coverage V1

Frozen before new simulation. Purpose: determine whether actual short-horizon requests provide moving, contact-free alternatives at the hard_0 E1 0004 start, where the prior fixed 4 m curve bank had no eligible choice. This is an offline action-conditioned measurement experiment, not a trained visual navigator or a navigation success-rate test.

Exactly eight independent processes, each fully reset to the unchanged manifest episode `hard_0_e1_0004`: first replay its saved B0 request blocks (24 blocks, 2.4 s), then seven primitives. Moving primitives request main velocity +2 or -2 rad/s with auxiliary request -30, 0, or +30 degrees; stop requests (0,0). Each primitive lasts at most 6.0 s (60 blocks at 10 Hz), ending at the first block with physical contact. No extra speed/angle/horizon sweep. Steering is named by actuator sign until the measured XY motion establishes its meaning.

Reuse formal `ball_action_v2_aux30`, runtime smoother, physical-contact-v2 sensing, frozen roll PID gains P=0.4/I=0.1/D=0.7, existing robot, camera rig, scene, fill lighting, and drive profile. All commands go through `runtime.execute` without bypassing execution limits. No state or smoother reset within a sequence. Capture 50 Hz physics state/raw/protocol/smoothed/applied commands and contact samples; capture both RGB/depth eyes at t=0, integer seconds, and termination. Rendered depth/map are offline diagnosis only, never request-generation inputs.

First require B0 replay to reproduce prior 25 XY/velocity/joint/applied samples and termination within 1e-6 absolute numerical tolerance, with identical contact sample booleans and step indices. If replay fails, stop before the seven primitive cases. Require all primitive starts to match B0 replay in root pose, measured velocities, joints, smoother state, and PID state. Invalid initial state or unavailable contact measurement makes a sequence unusable.

Freeze local outcome labels: `contact` if any measured contact; `insufficient` if sensor unavailable, failed runtime, or less than 6.0 s with no contact; `survived_moving` if the full 6 s has no measured contact and net XY displacement >=0.25 m; `survived_no_motion` otherwise. Stop is always separately reported and cannot count as a moving alternative. Coverage is present only if at this start at least one moving primitive survives 6 s and at least one moving primitive contacts. These labels certify only the measured horizon, not later safety or navigation. Report actual endpoint, signed displacement in the initial body frame, path length, goal-distance change, bank variation, and contacts separately.

The seven branches share a single initial scene and are not independent held-out scenes. Do not train a model or claim visual generalization from these eight runs. Preserve earlier candidate-bank rejection. Formal benchmark remains the original 35 s protocol; any 6 s termination here is explicitly diagnostic.
